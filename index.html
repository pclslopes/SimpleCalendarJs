Welcome to SimpleCalendarJs

A Js/Bootstrap fuelled calendar.



Powerful Simple Calendar Javascript Component

Streamline your scheduling and collaboration workflows with our feature-rich, customizable calendar.


Key Features

  • Month View: See all scheduled items across the month at a glance.
  • Week View: Zoom in on weekly tasks for detailed planning.
  • Day View: Focus on single-day priorities.
  • Year, Month, Week, and Day Selectors: Easily navigate between time frames.
  • Multi-Item Display: Track multiple overlapping events without clutter.
  • Customizable Appearance: Fully adaptable to your SaaS brand.
  • Drag-and-Drop Support: Rearrange tasks with ease.
  • Event Metadata: Add descriptions, links, and attachments for better context.
  • Responsive Design: Works seamlessly across all devices.
  • Integration-Ready: Compatible with popular backends and APIs.

How It Benefits Developers and Teams


For Developers

  • Easy-to-implement API and detailed documentation.
  • Save time with built-in features.
  • Extend functionality with custom hooks and events.

For Teams

  • Improve alignment with shared visibility of deadlines.
  • Enable seamless collaboration in real time.
  • Reduce scheduling conflicts by visualizing overlaps.
